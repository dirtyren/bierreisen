What this trip taught us

  • February in Franconia and Bavaria is quiet and cheap — but check Ruhetag, small breweries close Mondays.
  • Düsseldorf's Altbier houses pour faster than you can drink; the coaster on the glass is how you stop the round.
  • Trappist visits are appointment-only — La Trappe and Westvleteren both need planning weeks ahead.
  • Chimay's Auberge de Poteaupré is the best-value night of the trip: brewery, cheese and forest in one place.
  • The 6 h 54 Chimay → Zwiefaltendorf transfer is the one day worth splitting next time (Strasbourg or Baden-Baden).
