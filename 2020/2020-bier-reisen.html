What to drink where

One section per stop, ranked by how much the place is worth a detour — the breweries and bars that defined each night of this trip.

Munich (arrival)

01

Augustiner-Keller

Munich's oldest brewery and the locals' choice; Edelstoff from wooden barrels under the chestnuts.

02

Schneider Bräuhaus

The original wheat-beer house; Aventinus is the benchmark Weizenbock.

03

Hofbräuhaus

Brewing since 1589 — go once for the room.

04

Giesinger Bräu

Garage-to-glory craft brewery in the south; Erhellung unfiltered Helles.

Bamberg

01

Schlenkerla

Rauchbier since 1405, beechwood-smoked malt from oak barrels in a medieval vaulted cellar.

02

Spezial

The locals' rauchbier — subtler smoke than Schlenkerla.

03

Mahr's Bräu

The legendary 'aU' Ungespundetes, naturally carbonated and unfiltered.

04

Fässla

Zwergla dark lager, brewery tap opposite Spezial.

05

Greifenklau

Beer garden with the best view over the city.

Düsseldorf

01

Uerige

The reference Altbier: copper kettles, waiters who never stop pouring, and a house bitter enough to reset your palate.

02

Brauerei Schumacher

Oldest Altbier brewery in the city, blond and spritzy, in a tiled 19th-century hall.

03

Im Füchschen

Altbier and Rhineland food in the Altstadt's busiest corner.

04

Zum Schlüssel

Brewpub with a copper kettle in the cellar and a quieter room upstairs.

La Trappe

01

Brouwerij La Trappe

The only Dutch Trappist brewery, at Berkel-Enschot near Tilburg: Witte, Blond, Dubbel, Tripel and the barrel-aged Quadrupel.

Antwerp

01

Kulminator

700+ bottles with vintages going back decades; the cellar list is the reason to come.

02

Billie's Bier Kafétaria

300+ beers in a cosy neighbourhood room, staff who know the list.

03

De Koninck City Brewery

The Antwerp classic, Bolleke in its bowl-shaped glass.

Brussels

01

Cantillon

The last traditional lambic brewery inside the city: coolship, barrels, Gueuze and Kriek with no sweetening.

02

Moeder Lambic

40 taps of rare Belgian craft; the best introduction to the modern scene.

03

A la Mort Subite

1928 Art Deco café; order the house gueuze at the bar.

04

Poechenellekelder

Puppet-themed pub opposite Manneken Pis, 100+ Belgian beers.

Ghent

01

Gruut Stadsbrouwerij

City brewery using a medieval herb-and-spice mix instead of hops.

02

De Dulle Griet

500 beers on the Vrijdagmarkt, and the shoe-in-the-basket ritual.

03

Het Waterhuis aan de Bierkant

Canal-side beer hall, around 170 beers.

Bruges

01

De Halve Maan

Only active brewery in the historic centre; Brugse Zot and Straffe Hendrik, with the 3 km beer pipeline.

02

De Garre

Hidden alley café, house tripel at 11%, maximum three per person.

03

't Brugs Beertje

300+ bottles, the classic brown bar of Bruges.

04

Café Vlissinghe

Oldest pub in Bruges (1515), bocce court in the garden.

Westvleteren

01

In de Vrede

The café opposite Sint-Sixtus: the only reliable place to drink Westvleteren 12 on tap, with the abbey cheese.

02

Sint-Sixtus Abbey

The Trappist monastery itself — no tours, but the gate and the shop are part of the pilgrimage.

Chimay (Poteaupré)

01

Auberge de Poteaupré

The abbey's own inn and restaurant, a few hundred metres from Scourmont; Chimay Blue with abbey cheese.

02

Chimay Scourmont Abbey

The largest Trappist brewery; the Espace Chimay explains the brewing and the cheese.

Zwiefaltendorf

01

Blank's Brauerei

Village brewery and Gasthof on the Danube — Swabian Landbier, roast pork and no pretence.

Munich (return)

01

Augustiner-Keller

Wooden-barrel Edelstoff under the chestnuts; the locals' answer to every tourist beer hall.

02

Schneider Bräuhaus

Wheat-beer masters, Aventinus and the original Tap7.

03

Hofbräuhaus

The room everyone has heard of; go early or late.

04

Giesinger Bräu

Munich craft with a neighbourhood taproom.

05

Kloster Andechs (day trip)

Benedictine monastery brewery above the Ammersee — Doppelbock Dunkel, 45 km from the city.

What this trip taught us

  • February in Franconia and Bavaria is quiet and cheap — but check Ruhetag, small breweries close Mondays.
  • Düsseldorf's Altbier houses pour faster than you can drink; the coaster on the glass is how you stop the round.
  • Trappist visits are appointment-only — La Trappe and Westvleteren both need planning weeks ahead.
  • Chimay's Auberge de Poteaupré is the best-value night of the trip: brewery, cheese and forest in one place.
  • The 6 h 54 Chimay → Zwiefaltendorf transfer is the one day worth splitting next time (Strasbourg or Baden-Baden).
