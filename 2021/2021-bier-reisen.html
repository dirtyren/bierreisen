What to drink where

One section per stop, ranked by how much the place is worth a detour — from the Rauchbier town to the two brewery inns in the Allgäu and the fourteen Munich addresses on the list.

Bamberg

01

Schlenkerla

Rauchbier since 1405 — beechwood-smoked malt served from oak barrels in a vaulted cellar.

02

Spezial

The Bambergers' own rauchbier: the smoke is a whisper next to Schlenkerla.

03

Mahr's Bräu

The 'aU' Ungespundetes — unfiltered, naturally carbonated, the classic Franconian lager.

04

Fässla

Zwergla dark lager, brewery tap opposite Spezial, open late.

05

Klosterbräu

Bamberg's oldest brewery (1533); the Schwärzla is the one to order.

06

Greifenklau

Beer garden above the city with the best view of the roofs.

Nuremberg

01

Altstadthof

Hausbrauerei in the sandstone cellars under the castle — Rotbier and the rock-cut passages.

02

Schanzenbräu

Neighbourhood brewery on the hill; unfiltered Franconian styles, taproom with long tables.

03

Tucher Mautkeller

Big vaulted cellar restaurant, Rotbier on tap, convenient on a cold night.

04

Barfüßer Hausbrauerei

Brewpub by the river with copper kettles in the room and a young crowd.

Essing

01

Gasthaus Felsenwastl

The inn itself: Danube fish, local beer, and the river gorge at the door.

02

Kloster Weltenburg

Benedictine abbey 10 km downstream — Asamkirche and the oldest monastery brewery in Bavaria.

03

Befreiungshalle Kelheim

Not a brewery, but the walk up is worth the afternoon before dinner.

Traunstein

01

Hofbräuhaus Traunstein

Village-scale Hofbräuhaus with its own maltings; the Hell and the Doppelbock are the local benchmarks.

02

Brauerei Wieninger

Family brewery in Teisendorf, 15 km away — a half-day detour if the weather is bad.

Schönram (Petting)

01

Private Landbrauerei Schönram

One of Germany's most respected private breweries: Schönramer Pils is bitter, floral and dry — the reason to drive to Petting.

Berchtesgaden

01

Hofbrauhaus Berchtesgaden

The town's own brewery and restaurant; Helles and Dunkel brewed in the valley.

02

Grassl Distillery

Enzian and fruit brandies, for the non-beer half of the evening.

03

Königssee

Electric boat to St. Bartholomä — the scenery half of the two days.

Nesselwang

01

Brauerei-Gasthof Post

Brewery and Gasthof in one building: sleep upstairs, drink the house beer downstairs.

Missen-Wilhams

01

Brauereigasthof Schäffler

Allgäu village brewery with a wood-panelled Stube; a handful of beers, all served young.

Munich

01

Kloster Andechs

Benedictine monastery brewery above the Ammersee (45 km) — Doppelbock Dunkel and a terrace over the valley.

02

Weihenstephan, Freising

The world's oldest continuously operating brewery site (38 km); the Bräustüberl pours the standards.

03

Augustiner Bräustuben

The Augustiner brewery tap on Landsberger Straße: Edelstoff from the wooden barrel, meatloaf at the next table.

04

Augustiner Keller

Beer garden under the chestnuts; one of the few places still pouring from wooden barrels.

05

Chinesischer Turm

Englischer Garten beer garden at the pagoda — 7,000 seats, brass band, Maß in hand.

06

Viktualienmarkt

Market beer garden in the centre, with the Maibaum overhead and a rotating brewery tap.

07

Schneider Bräuhaus

Wheat-beer house on Tal: Aventinus, the original Tap 7, and a menu built for it.

08

Paulaner Brauhaus

The Nockherberg brewpub — Salvator season if you are lucky with the calendar.

09

Giesinger Bräu

Craft brewery in Giesing; Erhellung unfiltered Helles and a taproom full of locals.

10

Hopfenhäcker

Creative brewery founded 2016 in Haidhausen, moved into the Forschungsbrauerei in Perlach in 2021.

11

Forschungsbrauerei Bräustüberl

Perlach research brewery and taproom — the same address as Hopfenhäcker, so both in one stop.

12

Isartaler Brauhaus

Neighbourhood brewpub in the south of the city, house beers brewed on site.

13

Schweiger Bräu

Private brewery in Markt Schwaben, east of the city — find it on tap around town.

14

Wildbräu Grafing

Grafing's own brewery, almost a thousand years of history and eleven classic Bavarian beers.

Frankfurt Airport

01

Frankfurt Airport

What this trip taught us

  • Late October is a good call: the beer gardens are still open, the roads are empty and the hotels are cheap — but check Ruhetag, small breweries close Mondays.
  • The two brewery inns (Nesselwang's Post and Missen's Schäffler) are the best value of the trip: sleep above the brewhouse, drink what was brewed downstairs.
  • Berchtesgaden → Nesselwang at 280 km / 3 h 20 is the one transfer worth splitting — Garmisch or Tegernsee in between.
  • Munich in one block beats Munich in two: seven nights at Hotel Italia made the day trips (Andechs, Freising, Grafing) possible without packing.
  • Book Schönram around lunch — a 23 km detour for a Pils that bitter is worth the schedule change.
