Bamberg to the Allgäu, 2021

Eighteen days from the end of October 2021, moving every night or two: Bamberg, Nuremberg, a cave hotel in Essing, the mountains above Berchtesgaden, two village breweries in the Allgäu, and a week in Munich at the end to stop moving.

Nine beds, one suitcase

This was the trip that tested whether a different bed every night is a feature or a cost. The answer, by the sixth move, was cost — but the compensation was that each village brewery was a place you slept in, not a place you visited.

Essing set the tone: a hotel built into the rock above the Danube gorge, with the brewery tap downstairs and nothing to do after dark but drink. Traunstein and Nesselwang followed the same logic, smaller and quieter.

The Allgäu leg was the surprise. Missen-Wilhams and Nesselwang are not on any beer route; they are villages with a brewery and a guesthouse, and the beer is better than it has any right to be at that scale.

The Munich week

Seven nights in Munich at the end was the correction to 2020. Instead of a new town, a new list: Andechs, Freising and Weihenstephan, Wildbräu in Grafing, Schweiger in Markt Schwaben, the Forschungsbrauerei in Perlach, and the ordinary Munich rooms — Augustiner, Schneider, Paulaner, the Viktualienmarkt.

October turning into November is the best week for this: the beer gardens are still open on the good days, the chestnuts are roasting, and the city has not yet started Christmas.

What to order

  • Weihenstephan's Helles at the Bräustüberl in Freising, on the hill where the brewery has stood since 1040.
  • Wildbräu in Grafing, a brewery that has been family-run since 1589.
  • Schweiger's Hell at Markt Schwaben, fifteen minutes by S-Bahn from the city.
  • Anything from the Forschungsbrauerei in Perlach — the brewery that invented the yeast-bräu style and still argues about it.

Where to eat

  • The brewery dinner at Brauereigasthof Schäffler in Missen-Wilhams, where the menu and the beer come from the same building.
  • Sausages at the Viktualienmarkt stand, standing up, in the cold, which is the correct way.
  • Venison, in November, anywhere in the Allgäu that has it on the board.

Notes for travellers

  • The German hotel breakfast is the meal that makes one-night moves survivable; book rooms with it included and skip lunch.
  • Village breweries close their kitchens early and their taps earlier; arrive before eight or eat elsewhere.
  • Essing's rock hotel is worth the detour, but the gorge path is unlit: bring a torch for the walk back.
  • Berchtesgaden in early November can be closed for the season and open for the snow, sometimes on the same day.
