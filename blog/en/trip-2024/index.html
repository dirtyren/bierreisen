Franconia, Bohemia and Advent, 2024

Sixteen days from 20 November 2024: three nights above a Franconian brewery in Memmelsdorf, two in Plzeň, one with Budvar, two under the Watzmann, and five in Munich while the Christmas markets opened around the corner.

Advent as the reason

The last week of November is the quiet before the markets take over and the week when they are still worth walking through. Bamberg's are spread across the whole old town; Munich's are four separate markets with four separate crowds.

Brewery tours in late November are half empty, which means the guide has time and the tasting is generous. Drei Kronen in Memmelsdorf and Budvar in České Budějovice both ran that way.

The route went out through Frankfurt and came back through Rome, which sounds extravagant and was mostly a cheaper fare. It did mean landing in São Paulo the next morning with a suitcase of Rauchbier.

The Czech pair

Plzeň and České Budějovice are four hours apart by car and a century apart in character. Plzeň invented the pale lager everyone else copied; Budvar has spent a hundred years arguing with Budweiser about a name and making a slower, rounder beer while it argued.

One night in České Budějovice is not enough for the town, but it is enough for the brewery, the square and dinner. Two nights, as in 2026, is better.

What to order

  • Budvar's draught lager at the brewery, unpasteurised, which never travels as well as it tastes there.
  • Pilsner Urquell from the wood in the cellars at Plzeň, with the tour.
  • Drei Kronen's dark lager in Memmelsdorf, in the tap room, before dinner.
  • Glühwein is not beer, but at the Bamberg market in the cold it is the correct second drink.

Where to eat

  • Schäuferla in Bamberg, once, properly, with a Rauchbier.
  • Czech svíčková in Plzeň — beef, cream, cranberries — which is the meal the lager was designed around.
  • Roast goose, if you are in Bavaria in Advent; it is on every second menu and it is the season.

Notes for travellers

  • Christmas markets take cash and small notes; the Glühwein deposit mug is worth returning only if you do not want the mug.
  • Czech brewery tours often run in Czech and English on the same slot; book the English one rather than standing through both.
  • The drive from Plzeň to Berchtesgaden is long and crosses no useful brewery; take the motorway and use the evening.
  • Munich in the first week of December: book the hotel early, the city fills for the markets.
