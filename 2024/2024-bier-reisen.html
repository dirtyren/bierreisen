The route through Franconia & Bohemia

Numbered in visit order, from Frankfurt through Franconia and Bohemia to the Alps and back up to Munich. Rome, São Paulo and Porto Alegre are flight connections and sit outside this frame.

What to drink where

One section per stop, ranked by how much the place is worth a detour — two village breweries outside Bamberg, the town that invented pilsner, the other Budweiser, and an Advent in Munich.

Memmelsdorf

01

Brauerei & Gasthof Drei Kronen

Brewery, Gasthof and hotel in one building in Memmelsdorf — hand-brewed Franconian beer and a few rooms upstairs.

02

Brauerei & Wirtshaus Göller

Family brewery in Memmelsdorf-Drosendorf, a short drive out; unfiltered Franconian lagers in the Wirtshaus.

03

Schloss Seehof

The prince-bishops' summer palace at the edge of the village — gardens, orangery, and a walk before dinner.

04

Schlenkerla, Bamberg

Six kilometres away: Rauchbier since 1405, poured from oak barrels in a vaulted cellar.

05

Spezial, Bamberg

The Bambergers' rauchbier — softer smoke than Schlenkerla, and the locals' choice.

Plzeň

01

Pilsner Urquell (Prazdroj)

The brewery that defined the style in 1842: the tour ends in the cellars with an unfiltered, unpasteurised pour.

02

Na Spilce

The brewery's own restaurant under the brewhouse — the freshest Prazdroj you will get outside the cellar.

03

Purkmistr, Černice

Brewery and beer spa on the edge of town: baths in wort, a house lager, and a hotel if you overdo it.

04

Pivovarský dům

Brewpub in the centre with its own beers and a menu built around them.

05

Gambrinus

The everyday Plzeň lager, lighter and cheaper than Prazdroj, and just as local.

České Budějovice

01

Budweiser Budvar

The other Budweiser: tours of 70–80 minutes through the brewhouse and the cellars, with a tasting at the end.

02

Budvar Samson

The city brewery on náměstí Přemysla Otakara II — dark and light, in a 19th-century building.

03

Masné krámy

The old meat market turned beer hall, pouring Budvar by the metre.

Berchtesgaden

01

Hofbrauhaus Berchtesgaden

The town's own brewery and restaurant; Helles and Dunkel brewed in the valley.

02

Königssee

Electric boat to St. Bartholomä — the scenery half of the two days.

03

Grassl Distillery

Enzian and fruit brandies for the non-beer half of the evening.

Munich

01

Marienplatz Christkindlmarkt

The Advent market under the Glockenspiel; Glühwein in the cold, and the brewery stalls around the edges.

02

Tollwood

The winter festival on the Theresienwiese — food stalls, music and a long bar list.

03

Augustiner Bräustuben

The Augustiner brewery tap: Edelstoff from the wooden barrel, meatloaf at the next table.

04

Augustiner Keller

Chestnut beer garden with a winter room; still pouring from wooden barrels.

05

Hofbräuhaus

The room everyone has heard of — go early, stay for the brass band.

06

Schneider Bräuhaus

Wheat-beer house on Tal: Aventinus and the original Tap 7.

07

Viktualienmarkt

Market beer garden in the centre; in Advent the stalls turn to roasted almonds and Glühwein.

08

Giesinger Bräu

Craft brewery in Giesing with a taproom full of locals.

09

Kloster Andechs

Benedictine monastery brewery above the Ammersee (45 km) — Doppelbock Dunkel and a terrace over the valley.

10

Weihenstephan, Freising

The oldest brewery site in the world (41 km); the Bräustüberl pours the standards.

11

Forschungsbrauerei Bräustüberl

Perlach research brewery and taproom, with the craft crowd next door.

12

Paulaner Brauhaus

The Nockherberg brewpub — Salvator season if the calendar lines up.

Rome

01

Fiumicino connection

No beer stop: enough time for an espresso and the walk between gates.

What this trip taught us

  • Late November is the best-value week of the year: Christmas markets open, beer gardens close, prices drop — and the brewery tours are half empty.
  • Memmelsdorf is the quiet way to do Bamberg: two village breweries on the doorstep and the Rauchbier town six kilometres away.
  • Book Budvar and Pilsner Urquell in advance — in late November the English tours run once or twice a day.
  • České Budějovice → Berchtesgaden at 256 km / 3 h 05 is the long day; it crosses into Bavaria through the forest and there is nowhere to rush.
  • Five nights in Munich is the right length for Advent: one market day, one day trip, one museum day and two slow evenings.
