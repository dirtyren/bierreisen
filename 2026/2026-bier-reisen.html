The route through Franconia, Bohemia & the Alps

Numbered in visit order, from Frankfurt through Franconia and Bohemia to the Alps and back up to Munich. Porto Alegre is the flight and sits outside this frame.

What to drink where

One section per stop, ranked by how much the place is worth a detour — a Franconian brewing village, the town that invented pilsner, the other Budweiser, and three Alpine days.

Merkendorf

01

Brauerei Wagner

Family brewery in Merkendorf since 1797, Pointstraße 1 — Franconian lagers poured in the brewery tap a few steps from the kettle.

02

Drei Kronen, Memmelsdorf

Brewery, Gasthof and hotel 11 km away; hand-brewed Franconian beer and a full menu.

03

Metzgerei-Brauerei, Merkendorf

The village butcher who also brews — sausage, beer and a taproom in one building.

04

Schlenkerla, Bamberg

11 km to the Rauchbier cathedral: beechwood smoke, oak barrels, a vaulted cellar.

05

Spezial, Bamberg

The Bambergers' own rauchbier — softer smoke, shorter queue.

06

Mahr's Bräu, Bamberg

The 'aU' Ungespundetes, the reference Franconian lager.

Plzeň

01

Pilsner Urquell (Prazdroj)

The brewery that defined pilsner in 1842; the tour ends with an unfiltered pour in the cellars.

02

Na Spilce

The brewery restaurant under the brewhouse — the freshest Prazdroj in town.

03

Purkmistr, Černice

Brewery, hotel and beer spa; book the bath if you want the full production.

04

Gambrinus

The everyday Plzeň lager — lighter, cheaper, and just as local.

České Budějovice

01

Budweiser Budvar

The other Budweiser: 70–80 minute tours through the brewhouse and the cellars, tasting at the end.

02

Budvar Samson

The city brewery on the main square — dark and light, in a 19th-century building.

03

Masné krámy

The old meat market turned beer hall, Budvar by the metre.

Bad Reichenhall

01

Bürgerbräu Bad Reichenhall

Private Alpine brewery since 1494, Waaggasse 1–3 — the town's own beer, with a Brauereigasthof.

02

Brennerbräu

Gasthof between Bad Reichenhall and Berchtesgaden — the stop on the way back from Salzburg.

03

Hofbrauhaus Berchtesgaden

18 km away: the valley's own brewery and restaurant, Helles and Dunkel.

04

Augustiner Bräu Mülln, Salzburg

23 km across the border: the Mülln monastery brewery, beer from the wooden barrel, bring your own food.

05

Stiegl, Salzburg

Austria's largest private brewery, with a brewery tour and a museum in the city.

Munich

01

Kloster Andechs

Benedictine monastery brewery above the Ammersee (45 km) — Doppelbock Dunkel and a terrace over the valley.

02

Dachauer Brauerei

20 km out: the town's craft brewery, a short walk from the station and the memorial site.

03

Hofbräuhaus

The room everyone has heard of — go early, stay for the brass band.

04

Viktualienmarkt

Market beer garden in the centre, with a rotating brewery tap under the Maibaum.

05

Augustiner Bräustuben

The Augustiner brewery tap on Landsberger Straße: Edelstoff from the wooden barrel.

06

Augustiner Keller

Chestnut beer garden and winter room, still pouring from wooden barrels.

07

Schneider Bräuhaus

Wheat-beer house on Tal: Aventinus and the original Tap 7.

08

Die Residenz

The Wittelsbach palace — the day's walk ends here, with the beer halls of the Altstadt a few minutes away.

Beer guide

Four beer regions in sixteen days: Franconian smoke and village lagers, the Bohemian pilsner tradition from Plzeň to Budějovice, the Berchtesgadener Land and Salzburg, and Munich.

What this trip taught us

  • Late February is the quiet season: the brewery taps are open, the tourists are not, and the Alpine roads are empty.
  • Merkendorf is the deep end of Franconia: Wagner since 1797, a butcher who brews, and Bamberg eleven kilometres away.
  • Bad Reichenhall is the right base for three days of Alpine beer — Berchtesgaden is 18 km and Salzburg 23 km, so the car stays parked.
  • Two nights at Budvar beats one: the second morning is for the tour you could not get into the first day.
  • The 404 km Munich → Frankfurt leg on departure day is the longest of the trip; leave after breakfast and it is an easy afternoon.
