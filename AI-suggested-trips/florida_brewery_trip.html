🍺 Florida Brewery Trail

12 Days of Craft Beer, Hidden Gems & Classic Pubs
✈️ MIA → Miami → Orlando → Tampa → ✈️ TPA

🗓️ Your 12-Day Brewery Adventure

Arrive at Miami International (MIA) • Depart from Tampa International (TPA)
Route: Miami/Ft. Lauderdale (Days 1-4) → Orlando (Days 5-8) → Tampa/St. Pete (Days 9-12)

1
Arrival & Wynwood Warm-Up
📍 Miami - Wynwood Arts District
  • ✈️
    Arrive MIA
    Pick up rental car, settle into hotel
  • 🍺
    The Tank Brewing Co.
    5 min from airport! Start with La Playita Pilsner. Latin gastropub food + 18 taps. Perfect jet-lag cure.
  • 🍺
    J Wakefield Brewing
    World-class pastry stouts & fruited sours in the heart of Wynwood. Try DFPF and Miami Madness.
  • 🍺
    Veza Sur Brewing Co.
    Latin-inspired craft beer. Outdoor vibes. Mangolandia & Paleta de Guayaba are musts.
  • 🌃
    Wynwood Art Walk
    Stroll the murals between brewery stops. All 3 spots are walkable!
2
Doral Deep Cuts & Hidden Gems
📍 Miami - Doral / South Miami
  • 🍺
    Tripping Animals Brewing
    🔥 MUST VISIT! Florida's hottest brewery. Smoothie sours that sell out in minutes. Get there early for can releases.
  • 🍺
    MIA Beer Company
    Celebrating all things Miami. Tourist Trappe (Belgian Tripel, 9.5%) is exceptional. Warehouse vibes.
  • 🍺
    Spanish Marie Brewery
    Named after a Prohibition-era rum runner. Farmhouse ales & tropical fruited sours. True beer hunter's gem.
  • 🍺
    Unseen Creatures Brewing
    🌟 HIDDEN GEM! Foraged local wild yeast. Beers that are "inherently Miami." Farmhouse perfection.
  • 🍺
    Lincoln's Beard Brewing
    Beloved neighborhood spot in a strip mall. Don't let the location fool you - incredible beer & live music.
3
Coral Gables History & Miami Beach
📍 Miami - Coral Gables / Miami Beach
  • 🏛️
    Titanic Brewery & Restaurant
    Miami's OLDEST brewpub (since 1997)! GABF award-winner. $8 Burger Tuesday. Try Britannic Best Bitter.
  • 🍺
    Abbey Brewing Company
    Belgian-focused brewpub on Miami Beach. Authentic European pub feel away from the party scene. Try Brother Dan's Double.
  • 🏖️
    South Beach Break
    Walk the beach, grab lunch on Ocean Drive. You've earned it after 2 days of brewery crawling!
  • 🍺
    Boxelder Craft Beer Market
    Evening: Part nano-brewery, part bottle shop. Rare beer nerd paradise in Wynwood. 12+ curated taps.
4
Fort Lauderdale & Drive North
📍 Fort Lauderdale → Drive to Orlando
  • 🍺
    Funky Buddha Brewery
    LEGENDARY! Maple Bacon Coffee Porter changed craft beer. Massive taproom, full food menu. Morning Wood (annual) is a whale.
  • 🍺
    LauderAle Brewery
    Small-batch German/Belgian-inspired gems. Dog-friendly, intimate taproom. Quality over hype.
  • 🍺
    The Riverside Market
    Not a brewery but 300+ craft beers, 36 taps! Best craft beer bar in South Florida. Amazing deli sandwiches.
  • 🚗
    Drive to Orlando
    ~3.5 hours via Florida Turnpike. Arrive evening, check into hotel near Mills 50 / Ivanhoe area.
🚗 Drive Day: Fort Lauderdale → Orlando (~3.5 hrs via FL Turnpike)
5
Mills 50 & Virginia Drive Corridor
📍 Orlando - Mills 50 / Virginia Dr
  • 🍺
    Ten10 Brewing Company
    Trendy Virginia Dr. Excellent food pairings + craft beer. Try County Seat Amber & Virginia Drive IPA.
  • 🍺
    Sideward Brewing
    🔥 BEST sour program in Central FL! Cult can releases. Overnight Celebrity (Hazy IPA) is outstanding.
  • 🍺
    Ocean Sun Brewing
    Tiny nano-brewery, community vibes. Experimental small batches in trendy Mills 50.
  • 🍺
    Redlight Redlight Beer Parlour
    🌟 LEGENDARY! Sparked Orlando's craft beer revolution (~2005). No TVs. 30+ rare taps. Beer nerd Mecca.
6
Ivanhoe Village & College Park
📍 Orlando - Lake Ivanhoe / College Park
  • 🍺
    Ivanhoe Park Brewing
    Gold Medal winner! Beautiful Lake Ivanhoe setting. Park Hopp'r Pilsner won BFC Gold. Food trucks nightly.
  • 🍺
    Bowigens Beer Company
    Neighborhood charm, dog-friendly. Hop Soup (NE IPA) is delicious. Relaxed Ivanhoe Village vibe.
  • 🍺
    Tactical Brewing
    Military-themed nano in Baldwin Park. 20+ unique taps. Hop Doctrine IPA & Oorah! Stout are stellar.
  • 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿
    Braveheart Pub
    Authentic British pub in College Park. Real ales, proper fish & chips, whisky selection. Cozy evening spot.
7
SODO, Downtown & Hidden Spots
📍 Orlando - Downtown / SODO
  • 🍺
    Rockpit Brewing & Distilling
    Brewery + Distillery! Moonshine, vodka, gin + great beer. Live music. BBQ from Cecil's next door. RockPit Red is a gem.
  • 🍺
    Dead Lizard Brewing
    🌟 TRUE HIDDEN GEM! Industrial area, family-run. Great lagers at fair prices. Off-the-beaten-path find.
  • 🍺
    Orange County Brewers
    Downtown Orlando staple on Church St. Accessible, well-made beers. Great starting point.
  • 🏛️
    Wops Hops Brewing
    In a 1920s building! Part brewery, part history lesson. Prohibition Porter is aptly named.
8
Winter Park Gastro & Winter Garden
📍 Orlando - Winter Park / Winter Garden
  • 🍽️
    The Ravenous Pig
    🏆 JAMES BEARD NOMINATED! Farm-to-table gastropub with on-site brewery. Winter Park's finest. Book ahead!
  • 🍺
    Toll Road Brewing
    Hidden north of Orlando. German/Belgian focus in a sea of IPAs. Generous pours, laid-back vibes.
  • 🍺
    Crooked Can Brewing
    Inside charming Plant Street Market, Winter Garden. Florida Sunshine lager + food vendors + West Orange Trail access.
  • 🌳
    Winter Garden Downtown
    Walk the Garden Theatre district, grab dinner. Beautiful small-town Florida charm.
🚗 Drive Day: Orlando → Tampa (~1.5 hrs via I-4)
9
Cigar City & Seminole Heights
📍 Tampa - Seminole Heights
  • 🏆
    Cigar City Brewing
    ICONIC! #3 Brewer in the World (2010). Jai Alai IPA is legendary. Taproom-exclusive small batches. Don't skip this.
  • 🍺
    Angry Chair Brewing
    🔥 One of the BEST stout breweries in America. German Chocolate Cupcake Stout is life-changing. Dark, moody vibes.
  • 🍺
    Hidden Springs Ale Works
    Pastry sour specialists! Candy-themed names, Instagram-worthy. Cozy Tampa Heights taproom, dog-friendly.
  • 🍺
    Penelope (craft beer bar)
    Best curated tap list in Tampa. Staff knows their stuff. 20+ rotating taps from the best local/regional breweries.
10
Ybor City & Tampa Heights
📍 Tampa - Ybor City / Tampa Heights
  • 🍺
    Coppertail Brewing Co.
    Beautiful industrial-chic taproom near Ybor. Full kitchen! Unholy Trippel (9.5%) & Free Dive IPA are flagships.
  • 🏛️
    Tampa Bay Brewing (Ybor City)
    Operating since 1995! One of Tampa's OG brewpubs in the historic Centro Ybor. Old Elephant Foot IPA.
  • 🍺
    Magnanimous Brewing (Tampa Heights)
    True hidden gem. Creative small-batch beers. Also check their Seminole Heights location with arcade/pinball!
  • 🍺
    The Independent
    Iconic Seminole Heights bar. 30+ craft taps, vintage atmosphere. The heart of Tampa's beer community.
  • 🌃
    Ybor City Nightlife
    Explore the historic National Landmark District. Cuban sandwiches, cigar shops, 7th Avenue scene.
11
St. Petersburg Beer Paradise
📍 St. Petersburg - Downtown / EDGE District
  • 🍺
    Cycle Brewing
    🔥 WHALE ALERT! Tiny brewery, massive reputation. Barrel-aged stouts that trade at crazy value. Limited seating - go early!
  • 🍺
    Green Bench Brewing
    Beautiful beer garden + Webb's City Cellar next door (wild/sour program). Named after St. Pete's famous green benches.
  • 🍺
    3 Daughters Brewing
    Massive warehouse taproom! 250K+ visitors/year. Live music, games, food. Beach Blonde is a Florida classic.
  • 🏖️
    St. Pete Beach Sunset
    Catch sunset at one of America's best beaches. You've earned those beachside beers!
12
Dunedin, Motorworks & Departure
📍 Dunedin / Tampa → TPA Airport
  • 🏛️
    Dunedin Brewery
    Florida's OLDEST microbrewery (1996)! Where Cigar City's founder worked. Scottish heritage town, Pinellas Trail.
  • 🍺
    Arkane Aleworks
    Off-the-beaten-path Belgian specialist. Dubbels, Tripels, Czech lagers. A local's local brewery.
  • 🍺
    Motorworks Brewing (Tampa)
    Award-winning beer garden by the harbor. Pulp Friction (Grapefruit IPA) for the road. Great last stop.
  • ✈️
    Depart TPA
    Tampa International Airport. Pro tip: there's a Cigar City tap house inside the terminal!

💡 Trip Tips & Suggestions

🚗 Getting Around

  • Rent a car at MIA - essential for the brewery trail. Return at TPA (one-way rentals available)
  • Use Uber/Lyft for brewery crawl nights - NEVER drink and drive
  • Miami → Orlando: ~3.5 hrs via Florida Turnpike (tolls ~$15)
  • Orlando → Tampa: ~1.5 hrs via I-4 (notoriously congested)
  • St. Pete is 30 min from Tampa via I-275 across the bay
  • Dunedin is 30 min north of St. Pete - worth the drive

🏨 Where to Stay

  • Miami (Days 1-3): Wynwood or Brickell. Walking distance to breweries
  • Orlando (Days 5-8): Mills 50 / Ivanhoe Village - brewery dense corridor
  • Tampa (Days 9-12): Seminole Heights for beer corridor, or downtown St. Pete last 2 nights

🍺 Beer Strategy

  • Follow breweries on Instagram for can release announcements
  • Bring a cooler in rental car for take-home purchases
  • Ask bartenders what's "taproom only" - always the special stuff
  • Untappd app: check in, find nearby, read reviews
  • Visit Cycle Brewing EARLY (limited seating)
  • Tripping Animals releases on social media - check AM

🎯 Can't-Miss Experiences

  • Hunahpu's Day (March): Cigar City legendary release. 8000+ attendees!
  • Seminole Heights Crawl: Angry Chair → Magnanimous → Penelope → The Independent
  • Wynwood Art Walk: 1st Saturday monthly + J Wakefield + Wynwood Brewing
