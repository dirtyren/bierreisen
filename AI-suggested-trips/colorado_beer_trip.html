🗓️ 12-Day Itinerary Flowchart

1

Arrival Day - Denver

📍 Denver International Airport → RiNo District
✈️ Land at DEN → Check in hotel (RiNo/LoDo area)
🍺 Wynkoop Brewing Co - Colorado's first brewpub (est. 1988)
🍺 Great Divide Brewing - Barrel Bar for rare pours
💡 Tip: Take the A-Line train from DEN to Union Station ($10.50). RiNo is walkable from there. Wynkoop is right at Union Station - perfect jet lag cure.
2

Denver Deep Dive - RiNo & Five Points

📍 River North Art District & Five Points
🍺 Ratio Beerworks - Industrial chic, killer IPAs
🍺 Our Mutual Friend - Farmhouse ales & wild fermentation
🍺 Epic Brewing Denver - Big beers, barrel-aged stunners
🍺 Bierstadt Lagerhaus - German precision lagers, slow-pour pils
💡 Tip: Bierstadt's slow-pour Pilsner takes 5 minutes to pour and is life-changing. Their Lichtenhainer (smoked sour wheat) is incredibly rare to find on tap.
3

Denver - South Broadway & Baker

📍 SoBo / Baker Neighborhood
🍺 TRVE Brewing - Heavy metal + sours/wild ales
🍺 Baere Brewing - Tiny gem, mixed fermentation
🍺 Former Future Brewing - Neighborhood hangout, solid IPAs
🍻 Falling Rock Tap House - 75+ taps, legendary beer bar
💡 Tip: Falling Rock is a MUST - it's been the unofficial HQ of GABF week since 1997. If you only visit one beer bar in Colorado, this is it. Walk S. Broadway for vinyl shops between stops.
4

Denver - Hidden Gems Day

📍 Various Denver neighborhoods
🍺 Cerebral Brewing - Hazy IPAs & pastry stouts, top-tier
🍺 Westfax Brewing - West side sleeper, incredible lagers
🍺 Cohesion Brewing - Czech/European styles done perfectly
🍻 Euclid Hall (or Star Bar) - Historic Denver watering holes
💡 Tip: Cerebral is consistently rated top-5 in Denver. Their "Rare Trait" DIPA series is sought after. Cohesion is the real hidden gem - a tiny spot doing world-class Czech lagers.
5

Golden - Brewery Capital per Capita

📍 Golden, CO (25 min from Denver)
🍺 Coors Brewery Tour - Free tour of the world's largest single-site brewery
🍺 Golden City Brewery - Backyard patio, cash only, local legend
🍺 Cannonball Creek Brewing - Award-winning, best in Golden
🍺 Mountain Toad Brewing - Funky community spot
💡 Tip: Golden City Brewery's backyard is magical - feels like drinking at a friend's house. Cannonball Creek won GABF gold multiple times. Hike Lookout Mountain in the morning for views.
6

Golden + Transition to Boulder

📍 Golden → Boulder via Highway 93
🍺 New Terrain Brewing - Modern taproom, great views
🍺 Holidaily Brewing - 100% gluten-free, surprisingly excellent
🍺 Barrels & Bottles - Golden staple since 2014
🚗 Drive scenic Hwy 93 to Boulder (30 min)
💡 Tip: Hwy 93 between Golden and Boulder is one of Colorado's most scenic drives with Flatirons views. Stop at the overlook. Holidaily proves gluten-free beer can be world-class.
7

Boulder - Pearl Street & Legends

📍 Boulder, CO - Pearl Street Mall area
🍺 Mountain Sun Pub & Brewery - Cash only, no TVs, pure pub vibes since 1993
🍺 Avery Brewing Co - Massive taproom, barrel-aged beasts
🍺 Upslope Brewing - Canned adventure beers, chill patio
🍻 The Sink - Historic bar since 1923, Robert Redford worked here
💡 Tip: Mountain Sun is a Boulder INSTITUTION. No reservations, no credit cards, no pretense - just incredible house beers and a community vibe. Try the FYIPA. The Sink has the best burger in Boulder.
8

Boulder - East Side & Hidden Gems

📍 Boulder East / Gunbarrel / Flatiron Park
🍺 Wild Woods Brewery - Nature-inspired ales, all beers named after trees
🍺 Sanitas Brewing - Named after Mt Sanitas, great IPAs
🍺 Finkel & Garf Brewing - Science-forward, fun taproom
🍺 Bootstrap Brewing - Longmont local favorite, Insane Rush IPA
💡 Tip: Wild Woods is truly unique - their Treeline IPA and Ponderosa Porter use actual spruce tips. Hike Mt Sanitas in the morning (1.5hr) then reward yourself at Sanitas Brewing below.
9

Niwot + Longmont Detour

📍 Niwot & Longmont, CO (15-20 min from Boulder)
🍺 Fritz Family Brewers - Family-friendly, excellent lagers & ales (YOUR REQUEST!)
🍺 Left Hand Brewing - Longmont legend, Nitro Milk Stout pioneers
🍺 Wibby Brewing - All-lager brewery, outstanding
🍺 300 Suns Brewing - Longmont community gem
💡 Tip: Fritz Family in Niwot is a charming small-town brewery - great for a relaxed afternoon. Left Hand's Nitro Milk Stout on draft is incomparably smooth. Wibby does ONLY lagers and does them brilliantly.
10

Fort Collins - Old Town Legends

📍 Fort Collins, CO - Old Town (1hr from Boulder)
🍺 New Belgium Brewing - Iconic tour, Fat Tire birthplace
🍺 Odell Brewing Co - Employee-owned, phenomenal IPAs
🍺 Coopersmith's Pub & Brewing - FoCo's first brewpub (1989)
🍻 The Mayor of Old Town - 100+ taps, insane beer bar
💡 Tip: Book New Belgium tour in advance (free but fills up). Odell's taproom patio is one of the best in Colorado. The Mayor of Old Town has 100 taps of Colorado-only craft beer.
11

Fort Collins - Deep Cuts & Funk

📍 Fort Collins - Midtown & South
🍺 Funkwerks - Belgian-style saisons, GABF darling
🍺 Horse & Dragon Brewing - Neighborhood gem, Sad Panda stout
🍺 Zwei Brewing - German styles, excellent hefeweizen
🍺 Purpose Brewing - Peter Bouckaert's (ex-New Belgium) passion project
🍻 Road 34 Bike Bar - Bike culture + craft beer
💡 Tip: Purpose Brewing is run by the legendary Peter Bouckaert who created New Belgium's sour program. This tiny spot makes some of the most complex beers in Colorado. Funkwerks' Saison is a perfect 100 on many sites.
12

Departure Day - Last Stops & Airport

📍 Fort Collins → Denver (DEN Airport)
🍺 Snowbank Brewing - Morning coffee stout in FoCo
🚗 Drive back to Denver (1hr)
🍺 Denver Beer Co - Quick stop, Platte Street taproom
🛒 Grab cans at Argonaut Wine & Liquor (Denver's best bottle shop)
✈️ Head to DEN - grab a New Belgium or Breckenridge at terminal bars
💡 Tip: Argonaut on Colfax has the best Colorado craft selection to bring home. DEN has Great Divide and New Belgium taps in Concourse B. Allow 2hrs for security.

🏆 Complete Brewery & Pub Rankings

Ranked by overall experience: beer quality, atmosphere, history, and uniqueness. 💎 = Hidden Gem

1

Funkwerks

Fort Collins ⭐ 9.6/10
459 N College Ave, Fort Collins, CO 80524
GABF Small Brewery of the Year. Their Saison is world-class - perfectly balanced Belgian farmhouse ales. The taproom is intimate and the staff incredibly knowledgeable.
Mon-Thu 12-9pm, Fri-Sat 12-10pm, Sun 12-8pm
2

Avery Brewing Co

Boulder ⭐ 9.5/10
4910 Nautilus Ct N, Boulder, CO 80301
Massive 67-tap taproom with barrel-aged monsters. Their Barrel Series (Tweak, Rumpkin, Uncle Jacob's) is legendary. Restaurant on-site with food pairings.
Sun-Thu 11am-10pm, Fri-Sat 11am-11pm
3

Odell Brewing Co

Fort Collins ⭐ 9.5/10
800 E Lincoln Ave, Fort Collins, CO 80524
Employee-owned since 2015. Their IPA is the benchmark Colorado IPA. Massive patio, pilot system beers, and incredible consistency across their range.
Sun-Thu 11am-9pm, Fri-Sat 11am-10pm
4

Purpose Brewing

Fort Collins ⭐ 9.4/10
4025 S Mason St, Fort Collins, CO 80525
Peter Bouckaert's passion project after 20 years at New Belgium running their sour program. Tiny, intimate, wildly creative. Each visit has completely different beers.
Thu-Fri 4-8pm, Sat-Sun 2-8pm
5

Great Divide Brewing

Denver ⭐ 9.3/10
2201 Arapahoe St, Denver, CO 80205 (Barrel Bar)
Denver institution since 1994. The Barrel Bar in RiNo has rare barrel-aged pours. Yeti Imperial Stout and Titan IPA are Colorado classics. 24 GABF medals.
Sun-Thu 2-10pm, Fri-Sat 12-11pm
6

Bierstadt Lagerhaus

Denver ⭐ 9.3/10
2875 Blake St, Denver, CO 80205
Only makes 4 German lagers - and they're PERFECT. The slow-pour Pils takes 5 min and is transcendent. Decoction mashing, German-imported equipment. Purist heaven.
Mon-Thu 3-10pm, Fri-Sat 12-11pm, Sun 12-9pm
7

New Belgium Brewing

Fort Collins ⭐ 9.2/10
500 Linden St, Fort Collins, CO 80524
Home of Fat Tire. One of the first certified B-Corps in brewing. The tour is fantastic (includes river views and a slide!). La Folie sour and Voodoo Ranger series.
Sun-Thu 11am-9pm, Fri-Sat 11am-10pm
8

Mountain Sun Pub & Brewery

Boulder ⭐ 9.2/10
1535 Pearl St, Boulder, CO 80302
Cash only, no TVs, no Wi-Fi. Pure pub experience since 1993. FYIPA, Colorado Kind Ale, and rotating stouts are legendary. Tie-dye staff, Grateful Dead vibes.
Daily 11am-1am
9

Cerebral Brewing

Denver ⭐ 9.2/10
1477 Monroe St, Denver, CO 80206
Science-themed brewery with PhD-level brewing. Rare Trait DIPA series is cult-status. Pastry stouts and hazy IPAs are consistently top-rated on Untappd.
Mon-Thu 3-9pm, Fri 2-10pm, Sat 12-10pm, Sun 12-8pm
10

Cannonball Creek Brewing

Golden ⭐ 9.1/10
393 N Washington Ave, Golden, CO 80403
Multiple GABF gold medals. Small taproom, big flavors. Trump Hands (session IPA) and Solid Gold (golden ale) are crowd favorites. Best brewery in Golden.
Mon-Thu 2-9pm, Fri-Sat 12-10pm, Sun 12-8pm
11

Left Hand Brewing

Longmont ⭐ 9.1/10
1265 Boston Ave, Longmont, CO 80501
Pioneers of nitro bottle conditioning (Milk Stout Nitro). Large taproom with 24 taps. Fade to Black stout series is excellent. Employee-owned.
Daily 11am-9pm
12

Wynkoop Brewing Co

Denver ⭐ 9.0/10
1634 18th St, Denver, CO 80202
Colorado's FIRST brewpub (1988), founded by Governor John Hickenlooper. Historic LoDo building, pool hall upstairs. Rail Yard Ale is the classic.
Daily 11am-12am
13

Cohesion Brewing

Denver ⭐ 9.0/10
3851 Steele St, Denver, CO 80205
Tiny taproom doing Czech and European lagers with obsessive attention to detail. Unfiltered tank lagers, proper Czech Pils. If you love lagers, this is nirvana.
Wed-Fri 3-9pm, Sat 12-9pm, Sun 12-7pm
14

TRVE Brewing

Denver ⭐ 9.0/10
227 Broadway #101, Denver, CO 80203
Heavy metal-themed brewery doing exceptional sours and wild ales. Dark aesthetic, incredible mixed-fermentation program. Curse series is outstanding.
Mon-Thu 3-11pm, Fri-Sat 12-12am, Sun 12-10pm
15

Wibby Brewing

Longmont ⭐ 9.0/10
209 Emery St, Longmont, CO 80501
ALL-LAGER brewery - bold move that pays off brilliantly. Lightshine Helles, Moondoor Dunkel, and rotating IPLs. Huge outdoor area with games and food trucks.
Mon-Thu 11am-9pm, Fri-Sat 11am-10pm, Sun 11am-8pm
16

Ratio Beerworks

Denver ⭐ 8.9/10
2920 Larimer St, Denver, CO 80205
Industrial-chic RiNo staple. Hold Steady copper ale and Domestica kolsch are flagships. Huge patio, great music events. Approachable and consistent.
Mon-Thu 12-10pm, Fri-Sat 12-11pm, Sun 12-9pm

Horse & Dragon Brewing

124 Racquette Dr, Fort Collins, CO 80524
Neighborhood brewery with Sad Panda Coffee Stout as the standout. Small, friendly, and consistently excellent. Their Peach Sour in summer is divine.

Falling Rock Tap House

Boulder ⭐ 9.0/10
