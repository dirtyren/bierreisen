Complete Places Guide — Ranked

Roma (Days 1–3, Day 14)

Classic Historic Sites

#1The Colosseum & Roman Forum
Iconic amphitheater (70 AD). Combined ticket includes Palatine Hill. Book underground/arena floor tour.
Piazza del Colosseo, 1 — Book months ahead!
Classic
#2The Pantheon
Best-preserved Roman building (126 AD). Unreinforced concrete dome, world's largest for 2000 years. Oculus lets in rain and light.
Piazza della Rotonda — Free (reservation required since 2023)
Classic
#3Vatican City — St. Peter's & Sistine Chapel
Michelangelo's ceiling, Raphael's rooms, largest church in Christendom.
Vatican Museums, Viale Vaticano — Book skip-the-line
Classic
#4Trevi Fountain
Baroque masterpiece (1762). Visit at dawn (6am) for photos without crowds.
Piazza di Trevi
Classic
#5Castel Sant'Angelo
Hadrian's mausoleum turned papal fortress. Sunset rampart views over St. Peter's dome.
Lungotevere Castello, 50
Classic

Hidden Gems

#1Basilica di San Clemente
THREE layers — 12th-c basilica over 4th-c church over 1st-c Mithraic temple. Descend through 2000 years.
Via Labicana, 95 (near Colosseum)
Hidden Gem
#2Quartiere Coppedè
Fairy-tale neighborhood (1913-1926). Art Nouveau, Gothic, Baroque in surreal harmony. Almost no tourists.
Between Via Tagliamento and Piazza Buenos Aires
Hidden Gem
#3Aventine Keyhole
Look through a keyhole to see St. Peter's dome perfectly framed by garden hedges.
Piazza dei Cavalieri di Malta, Aventine Hill
Hidden Gem
#4Santo Stefano Rotondo
Circular 5th-century church, graphic martyrdom frescoes. Dickens: "a panorama of horror."
Via di Santo Stefano Rotondo, 7
Hidden Gem
#5Protestant Cemetery
Where Keats and Shelley rest. Pyramid of Cestius. Resident cats. Most atmospheric spot.
Via Caio Cestio, 6 (Testaccio)
Hidden Gem

💡 Tips & Suggestions

🚄 Transport Strategy

  • Buy a Trenitalia Pass or individual Frecciarossa tickets 2-3 weeks ahead (up to 70% cheaper)
  • Roma→Firenze: Frecciarossa 1h30, from €19 if booked early
  • Firenze→Pisa: Regional train 1h, €9 (no reservation needed)
  • Pisa→Milano: Frecciarossa ~3h (via Florence), €29-49
  • Milano→St. Moritz: Bernina Express 4h (UNESCO — DO THIS, book panoramic car, €70)
  • St. Moritz→Venezia: Train to Chur + EC to Venice, ~5.5h (€50-80)
  • Venezia→Roma: Frecciarossa 3h30, €30-60
  • Total transport estimate: €250-400 per person if booked ahead

📅 Day-by-Day Optimized Schedule

  • Day 1: Arrive FCO. Transfer to Trastevere hotel. Evening passeggiata + aperitivo.
  • Day 2: Colosseum/Forum/Palatine (AM). San Clemente + Coppedè (PM). Carbonara dinner.
  • Day 3: Vatican (AM — 7:30am entry). Castel Sant'Angelo. Aventine Keyhole (sunset). Ma Che beer.
  • Day 4: Train to Florence. Duomo climb + Uffizi (PM). Bistecca dinner. Archea beer.
  • Day 5: Accademia (AM). Oltrarno walk: Brancacci, San Miniato sunset. Lampredotto.
  • Day 6: Train to Pisa. Tower + Miracoli. Keith Haring mural. Cecina. Orzo Bruno evening. Sleep Pisa.
  • Day 7: Train to Milano. Duomo rooftop. Galleria. Navigli evening. Birrificio Lambrate.
  • Day 8: Last Supper (8:15am slot). San Maurizio. Brera. Risotto lunch. Lambiczoon (sour beers).
  • Day 9: Castello Sforzesco + Cimitero Monumentale. Shopping. Cotoletta dinner. Bernina prep.
  • Day 10: Bernina Express to St. Moritz. Afternoon: town walk + Leaning Tower. Fondue. Engadiner beer.
  • Day 11: Muottas Muragl sunrise. Val Roseg hike or Diavolezza. Nusstorte. Capuns dinner.
  • Day 12: Train to Venice. Arrive noon. Rialto Market. Cicchetti crawl. Scala del Bovolo. Sunset Grand Canal.
  • Day 13: San Marco + Doge's Palace (AM). San Giorgio campanile. Ghetto. Libreria Acqua Alta. Nero di seppia dinner.
  • Day 14: Morning train to Roma. Quick Trastevere walk. Final espresso. FCO departure.
